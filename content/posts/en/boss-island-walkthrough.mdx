# Boss & Island Walkthrough

How to Fish progresses through a chain of dangerous islands. The reliable route is to complete the island objective, gather the required bait or quest item, prepare for the boss, and only then move forward.

## General preparation

- Confirm the hand-in target and any bait requirement before leaving port.
- Keep enough inventory space for quest items and a profitable catch.
- Bring an upgrade that addresses the island’s main bottleneck.
- Use developer announcements for current patch behavior.

## Follow the progression order

Finish the main story first on your initial run. This naturally exposes new shops, lures, weapon attachments, boats, bosses, and systems. After the ending, return to the islands for optional achievements, trick shots, gambling, cooking, and collection cleanup.

<Callout type="tip">If a boss becomes the blocker, upgrade for the fight or switch the objective order only when the game allows it; do not discard progression items.</Callout>

## After each boss

Check the unlocked shop, system, and route back to the previous hand-in. That prevents a new island from becoming a long detour and keeps the next run profitable.
