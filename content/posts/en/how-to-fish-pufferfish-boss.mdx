# How to Fish Pufferfish boss

**The Pufferfish is the Desert-island boss in the Tourist quest chain: obtain a valid endangered creature for the Tourist, use the Carrot as bait, fight the Pufferfish, and respect its poison clouds.** The collected evidence confirms the Carrot route and poison phase, but exact HP and damage values are **to be confirmed**.

This page intentionally excludes unrelated Pufferfish results for Minecraft, Stardew Valley, real-life fish keeping, and cooking. Everything here is for the Dazed Games Steam game *How to Fish* and reflects evidence checked on August 28, 2026.

## Where the fight fits

The Desert island is the third stop on the evidence-based route: Lighthouse → Forest → Desert → Rocks → Volcano. This island adds Standard lures, the SMG, attachments, the grill, the casino, and the Tourist quest. The Pufferfish is part of progression, not an optional early boss.

Evidence summarizes the chain as “Tourist, endangered creature, Carrot, and Pufferfish.” That means you should not fight blindly after arriving. First identify the Tourist’s request, obtain the required creature or bait, and only then trigger the fight.

## Preparation before you trigger it

Bring a ranged option. The Desert island offers the SMG, and later evidence recommends improving bullet damage and magazine size. A poison-heavy boss punishes melee mistakes, so a ranged weapon gives you room to move while still dealing damage.

Bring healing or food. Evidence describes hunger as a reserve health layer, so enter the fight with the hunger bar high and, if possible, a healing creature available. Do not fill your entire inventory with weapons and bait before a fight that may require retreat and retry.

Also finish the easy Desert upgrades first. Evidence highlights the Boat Radar upgrade before leaving this island. Even if the Pufferfish is your current blocker, the upgrade helps the rest of the route and prevents navigation problems after the hand-in.

## Carrot bait and quest order

The evidence table lists the Pufferfish on Island 3 with Carrot as required bait. It also says the story follows the Tourist, endangered creature, Carrot, and Pufferfish chain. Treat the Carrot as a quest item until the fight is triggered successfully.

If you are following a speedrun-style route, the evidence explicitly says to catch one valid endangered creature, give it to the Tourist, and use the Carrot to defeat the Pufferfish. Do not skip the hand-in. After the fight, completing the Tourist objective is what keeps progression moving. Leaving with the boss dead but the trophy or proof unreturned is a common avoidable delay.

## How to fight the poison phase

The collected GamesRadar material identifies the Pufferfish fight with poison clouds. The safe rule is to treat the visible cloud as an area denial zone, not a place to squeeze in one more shot. Move out first, then resume damage when the safe path is clear.

Use ranged attacks while the boss is turning or transitioning. Evidence associates the fight with SMG fire and purple poisonous clouds, so sustained damage from outside the cloud is safer than standing inside for burst damage. Exact optimum DPS setup is **to be confirmed**.

Keep terrain in mind. Open ground gives you room to circle, but may also expose you to longer chase lines. Evidence from other bosses recommends having an escape lane; for the Pufferfish, the same principle applies when poison fills part of the arena.

## Healing and retry rules

Heal before the hunger bar empties, not after. Because hunger acts as a reserve health layer, eating during a safe movement window can prevent a death later. If the fight gives you a pause, use it to restore health instead of taking one extra risky shot.

If you die, don’t immediately change the entire build. First check whether you stood in poison too long, failed to keep distance, entered with low hunger, or lacked ammunition. Evidence does not prove one required weapon, so repeated deaths usually mean spacing rather than an item problem.

If the fight repeatedly stalls, improve the SMG path or return after unlocking more attachments. The broader guide evidence recommends story-first progress and controlled cleanup, so it is acceptable to complete another available objective only if that does not consume the Carrot or quest item.

## Common Pufferfish mistakes

Don’t sell or eat the quest creature before the Tourist hand-in. Don’t treat the Carrot as spare inventory. Don’t enter with a full inventory that leaves no room for healing or recovery items.

Don’t chase the boss into poison. The cloud is part of the fight’s damage, and evidence gives no confirmed invulnerability trick. Patience plus ranged damage is safer than inventing a bypass. Any exact cloud duration is **to be confirmed**.

Finally, don’t confuse this Pufferfish with unrelated game bosses. The search term attracts Minecraft, Stardew Valley, Palworld, and cooking results. Those mechanics do not apply to this Steam game.
