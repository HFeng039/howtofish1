## Why controller support is not Xbox

Steam lists full controller support, and the platform evidence specifically mentions Xbox, DualShock, and DualSense controllers on the Windows version. This is easy to misread: a wired or wireless controller on PC does not imply a console port.

An Xbox controller working on PC only proves input compatibility. An Xbox version would require an announced console build, store distribution, certification, and platform support. Those items are not present in the collected sources.

So if your goal is to play on a television, the evidence-supported PC options are a Windows PC connected to the display or a Steam Deck/PC handheld setup. Casting or remote-play behavior for this specific game is **to be confirmed**.
