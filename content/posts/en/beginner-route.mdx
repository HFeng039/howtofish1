# Beginner Route

How to Fish opens into one repeatable loop: catch creatures, sell the results, buy the upgrade that removes your current bottleneck, and hand in the next quest. Keep the first run focused on that loop before chasing optional collectibles.

## First priorities

1. Learn the basic catch and release controls until they feel deliberate.
2. Sell catches early enough to keep funds available for useful purchases.
3. Buy the rod, weapon, or boat upgrade that unlocks the next objective.
4. Complete quest hand-ins before leaving an island.

<Callout type="tip">If a purchase does not unlock progress, improve mobility, storage, or combat first.</Callout>

## Avoid common early mistakes

Do not spend limited early money on cosmetic or high-risk purchases. Do not ignore quest requirements; they often unlock the systems that make later runs faster. Finally, avoid carrying optional items through boss routes when the inventory space could support the progression.

## Next run

Once the opening route is stable, move into money and gear planning. That keeps the early two hours focused on income, upgrades, and the first bosses instead of repeated cleanup.
