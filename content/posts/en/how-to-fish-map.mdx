# How to Fish map

**The How to Fish map route is Radar-driven: Lighthouse → Forest → Desert → Rocks → Volcano.** Buy the Radar, sail to the next colored destination only after the current NPC hand-in updates it, and buy the Boat Radar upgrade on the Desert island before continuing. This page filters unrelated map results for Minecraft, Palworld, Stardew Valley, real-world fishing spots, and other games. It uses collected *How to Fish* evidence checked on August 28, 2026. If a patch changes dots or objectives, official Steam announcements are the newer source.

## Why the map is progression-gated

The map is not a free open-world checklist. Evidence describes five main island stops and says each stage ends with an NPC hand-in, boss proof, or radar update. This makes the route predictable even though the game’s physics are chaotic.

The broad order is Lighthouse, Forest, Desert, Rocks, and the military/Volcano area. A new destination should be treated as locked until the current hand-in is complete. Otherwise, you may reach a visible area without the bait, boat upgrade, or story trigger needed to progress.

The Radar gives direction, but progression gives permission. Evidence repeatedly pairs the next island with a new dot or coordinate update, so check both your current objective and your Radar before spending time sailing.

## Radar steps at the Lighthouse

The first navigation item appears after the Spider Crab chain. Evidence says to return the shell to the Lighthouse Keeper, receive the boat keys, buy the Radar tablet for $10, and board the wooden motorboat. Don’t sail before this sequence; the boat and Radar together unlock travel.

The first destination from the Lighthouse is the green dot on the Radar, which leads to Island 2, the Forest. This is also the best time to learn how the boat handles before carrying quest trophies and expensive gear.

Keep your first trips short. Learn where the dot sits relative to your starting position, then return if you forgot bait, food, or inventory space. The Radar is useful, but it does not replace preparation.

## Island dots through the route

After the Forest’s Giant Piranha hand-in, evidence says the woman updates the Radar with the yellow dot leading to Island 3, the Desert. Complete the hand-in and buy the Big Motor upgrade before making that trip.

The Desert island is also where evidence highlights the Boat Radar upgrade. Buy it before leaving. This upgrade mounts Radar on the boat so navigation is less dependent on returning to a tablet, and it prepares you for longer late-game routes.

From Desert to Rocks, progression continues through the Tourist/Pufferfish chain and next Radar update. From Rocks to Volcano, evidence describes the Tuna → Albatross hand-in and then sailing to the magenta dot. The exact colors for every intermediate step are **to be confirmed** outside the cited route.

## Route checkpoints

Before sailing, confirm three things: current hand-in complete, required trophy or bait protected, and next upgrade purchased. This prevents the common mistake of arriving at the next island without enough inventory or weapon power.

At the Lighthouse, complete the Spider Crab shell hand-in before taking the boat keys. At Forest, return the Giant Piranha skeleton before following the yellow dot. At Desert, finish the Tourist/Pufferfish chain and buy the Boat Radar upgrade before moving on.

At Rocks, evidence says to deliver the Albatross head to the man hiding in the shop before sailing to the magenta dot. At Volcano, progression is not complete until the Bowhead Whale chain, final mutation, Whale Fin hand-in, black boat, and return sequence are all finished.

## Use coordinates without inventing them

Evidence mentions using Radar coordinates to know when a stage is complete, but it does not provide a complete coordinate table. Treat coordinate lists from unrelated games or secondhand screenshots as **to be confirmed**.

A safer method is to record what your own Radar shows after each hand-in. Note the dot color, direction from your current dock, and boat upgrade used. That creates a repeatable route without publishing uncertain numbers.

If you play co-op, agree on one navigation caller. The host or designated navigator should confirm the next dot while others manage bait, healing, and boss roles. This reduces players sailing in different directions after a fight.

## Boats, upgrades, and travel safety

Buy movement upgrades when the route becomes slow. Evidence specifically mentions the Big Motor upgrade after the Forest and later Double Engine upgrades in Desert/Rocks shops. Extra speed saves time, but it is not a substitute for the Radar update.

Inventory space also affects navigation. Evidence recommends buying Extra Inventory Slot upgrades as islands unlock. A full inventory can force you to sell a quest item or return to port, turning a short trip into a long detour.

Before long trips, keep food and healing available. Hunger acts as a reserve health layer, so travel after eating rather than starting a boss approach with an empty hunger bar.

## Map-related objectives you may miss

The Desert island has the Boat Radar upgrade and also important progression systems: Standard lures, SMG, attachments, the grill, casino, and Tourist quest. Don’t leave just because the next dot is visible.

The Rocks island includes Professional lures, the Sniper Rifle, the casino, and the Tuna/Albatross route. These systems support later cleanup, even if your immediate goal is the Volcano.

The Volcano island uses lava cooking and a military shop with the Assault Rifle. Evidence describes five fish for the Scientist, Fish Bucket bait, the Bowhead Whale, and the Mutated Bowhead Whale sequence. The map route is not finished until the final hand-in and black-boat return.

## Common map mistakes

Don’t leave before the hand-in. A dead boss is not the same as a completed objective if the trophy, shell, skeleton, head, fin, or proof is still unreturned.

Don’t sell unusual bait or trophies to clear inventory. The route depends on specific triggers such as the Empty Beer Can, Leeches, Carrot, Tuna, and Bowhead Whale. If inventory is full, buy or unlock more capacity instead.

Don’t rely on map answers from Minecraft, Palworld, Stardew Valley, or real fishing content. Those search results share words but not mechanics.

Don’t assume exact coordinates from older videos still work. Evidence notes that Patch 1.0.5 removed a shortcut to locked islands, so normal progression is the reliable route on the current version.
