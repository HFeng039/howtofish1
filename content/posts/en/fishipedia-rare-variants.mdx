# Fishipedia & Rare Variants

The Fishipedia and collection achievements reward organized play. Ordinary creatures, rare Drip variants, locations, rods, lures, and boss catches all feed into the same long-term checklist.

## How to organize it

1. Finish the main story first so every island, shop, and system is reachable.
2. Track common creatures during normal routes.
3. Mark rare Drip variants separately because their distinctive look and rainbow-style naming make them easy to recognize.
4. Return after the ending for boss catches and final collection cleanup.

## Rare Drip variants

Drip creatures are the collection layer most likely to require a dedicated pass. When one appears, prioritize it if your cargo and funds allow; otherwise mark its location for a later cleanup route.

<Callout type="warning">Collection goals can conflict with a strict speedrun or story route. Separate those runs instead of forcing one attempt to serve both.</Callout>

## Final cleanup

There are 28 achievements in total. After completing the story, use your remaining checklist to group combat, cooking, casino, money, collection, and challenge tasks into efficient return trips.
