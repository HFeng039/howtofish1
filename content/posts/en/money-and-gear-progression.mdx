# Money & Gear Progression

How to Fish rewards players who turn each expedition into a complete profit loop. Catches become money, trick shots can increase that return, and money becomes the rod, weapon, attachment, or boat upgrade that opens the next objective.

## Build the loop

1. Plan a route that includes valuable catches and quest hand-ins.
2. Sell before purchases so your buying decision is based on real funds.
3. Buy the upgrade that removes your current blocker rather than another marginal item.
4. Repeat the route once the bottleneck moves.

## Trick shots as income

Trick shots are not only a spectacle. During the first two hours, they can help fund stronger equipment by adding profit to catches you were already making. Practice after securing a safe route, not while a boss or high-value cargo is at risk.

<Callout type="warning">Avoid sinking all funds into a single weapon before confirming that the next island objective needs combat, mobility, bait capacity, or storage instead.</Callout>

## Review every purchase

After each run, check whether money limited progress, whether an upgrade sat unused, and whether the next island needs a different tool. This keeps equipment purchases aligned with the route.
